## v0.6.0: use the mouse as your hands, shrink, walk around, and bring your own 3D rooms

### 🖱 Your mouse is your hand
Press on her to lay your hand on her and stroke her head. Shift or the right button uses the left hand; left and right click together is a pinch (pick her up). Number keys **1 open hand, 2 poke, 3 pick up, 4 tickle**.

### 🎥 Look around and walk
**Q / E** change the camera height, **C** is free roam (walk with the **arrow keys**, drag to look, Shift to run).

### 🔬 Shrink ray
A new shrink ray button and a shrink meter make you smaller, down to lollipop size. She looks down at you and reacts, and the room towers over you.

### 🏠 Bring your own 3D rooms
Import a house or room model (`.glb`, `.gltf` or `.fbx`, for example from Booth) in a room's settings. The app finds the floor, centres it, and she walks around the model and steps around its furniture. Set the size, turn and height if needed.
Each model has a sharing mark: **Not allowed to share** (default) or **May be shared**. Only models you mark as shareable go into an exported profile, and the export tells you which ones it left out. The mark is your own declaration, the app can't check a seller's licence.
A `.unitypackage` isn't opened yet (export the model as `.fbx` or `.glb` first). Unity shaders and baked lighting aren't carried over.

### 📱 Holding things properly
She holds her phone palm up with the screen toward her eyes, and her mug by the handle with her fingers through it.

### 🗂 Characters
Drag a character's card to put your characters in any order (top level and inside every folder). A folder with no picture can show the picture of one of its characters.

### Profiles on other devices
Profile files from the desktop version should now import on mobile (the file is read in pieces, and the file picker no longer hides the `.dorms` file). **I couldn't try this on a real phone, so please tell me if it still doesn't work.**
