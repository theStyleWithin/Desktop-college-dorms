## v0.11.0: Pathways, rooms that connect and a story that walks through them

### 🚪 Build a place out of rooms
A room can now be **part of another room**. Make "Our house", give it a picture and describe it, then make a Kitchen, a Living room and an Attic inside it, each with its own picture and description. In the room form, the new **Pathways** section has **This room is part of** and **Also connects to**, which is a doorway to anywhere else, like the garden gate. Doorways work both ways. Your Rooms list shows where each room sits and how many rooms are inside it.

### ➜ Walk the story from room to room
Next to the message box there's a new **Go to…** menu. Pick a room and the **same chat** carries on there. No new chat is made, and the conversation so far stays. A line in the chat marks the move, and the room's picture and music (if it has its own) change with it.
- The menu lists **nearby** rooms first (rooms inside this one, the place this room is part of, its neighbours and its doorways), then all your other rooms, so you're never stuck.
- The characters are told where they are now, what that place is part of, and where they can go next. The move is also told to them as part of the story, so when a character says "let's go make coffee in the kitchen", you can follow with one click.
- Deleting a room never loses anything: chats that were in it go back to their dorm's room, and rooms that were inside it move up a level.
- Rooms, their doorways and the room each chat is in are part of backups.

### What I couldn't test
I haven't tried a long roleplay through a real model with a move in the middle, or restoring a backup that has pathways in it. The move itself, the menu, the prompt the characters get, deleting rooms and the rest are tested.
