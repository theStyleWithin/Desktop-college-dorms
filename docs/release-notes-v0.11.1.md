## v0.11.1: choose who hangs in the picture frame

The side bar's **Picture frame** no longer just follows your latest chat. Click the **✎ pencil** on the frame and choose any character: they stay in the frame until you change it, however many chats you start with others. Clicking the frame opens your chat with them (or starts one). Prefer the old way? Choose **Whoever I talked to last**.

The choice is kept on each device.
