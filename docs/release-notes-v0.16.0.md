## v0.16.0: The Gallery Update 🖼️

### 🖼️ Gallery
A new page in the side bar, right after Characters, for when you just want to look at the art and not chat. (If you had arranged your side bar, the Gallery is slotted in after Characters once; drag it anywhere you like.)

- **An art folder for every character**, with their profile picture as the cover, how many pictures it holds, and "gallery of N" if they have a gallery of their own. A search box and an "only characters with a gallery" switch narrow the list, and the folders follow your character order.
- **Open a folder** to see the profile picture, and the character's own gallery as a **sub folder** inside. Click any picture to see it big: the arrow keys or buttons move through the folder's pictures, Escape closes it, and **Open character** jumps to the editor. Pictures, GIFs and short clips all work (a clip plays while the pointer is on it).
- **Add a folder from my computer** (desktop only) puts any folder of pictures on your computer in the Gallery under **From my computer**, with nothing to do with any character. Its subfolders show up as folders inside, and everything opens in the same viewer. It is only for looking: nothing is copied, changed or deleted, and the ✕ on a folder only takes it off the Gallery. For safety the app won't add a whole drive, your whole home folder, a hidden or system folder, or its own data folder, and it lists the first 5,000 pictures of a folder.

### 💬 Comments under every picture
Every picture in the big viewer has its own comment section, like a forum thread, for your own notes about a picture you like.

- Write **as many comments as you like** on a picture, whenever you like, and come back to add more.
- **Reply** to any comment (and to replies; they are shown indented), **edit** your comments, or **delete** them (deleting one asks first and takes its replies with it). A picture with comments shows a 💬 count.
- They stay **on your device only**, per profile, and travel with **backups and Wi-Fi transfers**. A comment can be up to 20,000 characters; a picture has no limit on how many.

### 🔗 Pictures from a link
- **Avatars**: in the character editor, next to Upload, paste a picture link and press **Use link** (on a web page, right-click a picture and choose Copy image address). You can also paste a picture you copied from the internet anywhere in the Avatar section. The picture then goes through the same positioning window and saving as an upload.
- **Galleries**: the same link box adds pictures to a character's own gallery, in the character editor and inside a character's folder in the Gallery.
- The app fetches the link itself (web addresses only, 40 MB at most), decides what the file is by the file itself rather than the server's label, and tells you plainly when a link is a web page, not a picture. A fetch starts only when you press Use link.

### Not yet
- Thumbnails (big photos are loaded at full size, lazily), a slideshow, and comments on a picture that carry their own pictures or formatting.
- The comments of a picture from a folder on your computer are tied to where the file is; if the file is moved, they no longer find it.

### What has not been tried
These were tested with the app's own tests and rendered pictures. Not tried: the real folder chooser, Windows folder paths, a very long comment thread, the comment panel on a phone-sized screen, video clips in the viewer, and pasting a copied picture straight from a browser. The Android in-app model and Wi-Fi transfer between real devices are still untested on hardware, as before.
